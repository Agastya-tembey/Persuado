Our Vision

To make high-quality Model United Nations preparation accessible to every aspiring delegate, regardless of experience, school, or location, by providing professional resources, practical guidance, and an inclusive learning platform.

02

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower thousands of Model United Nations delegates each year by making high-quality preparation, guidance, and research accessible to everyone. Persuado is committed to helping at least 1,000 delegates annually develop the confidence, knowledge, and practical skills needed to succeed in Model United Nations.

03

Why Persuado Exists

Persuado was created to remove barriers to quality Model United Nations preparation. We believe every delegate deserves access to reliable research tools, structured guidance, and professional resources without financial or geographical limitations.
