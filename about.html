About Us

Hi, I'm Agastya, a Grade 10 student from Cape Town, South Africa, and an aspiring International Law student with a passion for diplomacy, global affairs, public speaking, and Model United Nations.

I created this platform to make MUN preparation simpler, more organized, and more accessible for students. By bringing together reliable research tools, country data, UN resources, and practical guidance in one place, my goal is to help delegates prepare more effectively and participate with confidence.

Through this platform, I hope to inspire students to develop critical thinking, leadership, and diplomacy skills while engaging with the world's most pressing challenges.
